# Kessick linkspan fails load test, ferry start in doubt

*Coastal Gazette, 12 June 2026*

The new vehicle linkspan at Kessick failed its load test on Tuesday, putting the 1 July start of the Dunmore-Kessick
ferry in doubt. Saltmarsh Ferries said it would announce new dates within a week.
