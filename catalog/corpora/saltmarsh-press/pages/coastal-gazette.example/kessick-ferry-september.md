# Kessick ferry on course for September start

*Coastal Gazette, 12 September 2026, by our harbour correspondent*

Passengers will finally be able to sail from Dunmore to Kessick from 15 September 2026, Saltmarsh Ferries has said.
The route, first promised for July, was delayed when the new linkspan failed a load test.

Lorries have used the crossing since 1 August. Kessick traders told the Gazette they expect a busy autumn once
foot passengers arrive.
