# Kessick route: new start dates

*Press release, 18 June 2026*

The new linkspan at Kessick did not pass its load test in June, so the Dunmore-Kessick crossing will not open on
1 July as planned.

- Freight sailings will start on **1 August 2026**, once the linkspan is cleared for lorries.
- The first passenger sailing moves to **15 September 2026**.

Customers who booked for July will be refunded in full or moved to a date of their choice.
