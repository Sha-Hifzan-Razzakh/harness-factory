# Summer timetable on the Dunmore-Arlo crossing

*Press release, 2 May 2026*

From 15 June to 15 September 2026 the Dunmore-Arlo crossing runs six return sailings a day, two more than in
winter. The first sailing leaves Dunmore at 06:30.
