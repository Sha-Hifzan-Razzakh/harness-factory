# A new crossing: Dunmore to Kessick

*Press release, 10 March 2026*

Saltmarsh Ferries will open a new crossing between Dunmore and Kessick this summer. The route will start carrying
passengers on **1 July 2026**, with four return sailings a day on the MV *Grey Heron*.

The crossing takes 55 minutes. Kessick's new terminal, with a linkspan for vehicles, is being built by the Kessick
Harbour Trust.

Timetables and fares will be published in May.
