# Kessick: sea trials complete, first passenger sailing confirmed

*Press release, 5 September 2026*

Freight has been crossing between Dunmore and Kessick since 1 August. The MV *Grey Heron* finished her passenger
sea trials this week, and the maritime authority asked for two more weeks of crew drills at the new terminal.

The first passenger sailing on the Dunmore-Kessick crossing will now be on **29 September 2026**, two weeks later
than the date we gave in June. Bookings for 15 to 28 September will be moved to the same sailing on or after
29 September, or refunded.

This date is confirmed: the maritime authority has signed off the terminal for passengers from 29 September.
